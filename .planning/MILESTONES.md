# Milestones

## v1.0 Level 1 Adventure + Email Funnel (superseded)

**Started:** 2026-06-11 · **Closed:** 2026-10-07 (superseded by the v2.0 pivot, not completed)
**Archive:** `milestones/v1.0-ROADMAP.md`, `milestones/v1.0-REQUIREMENTS.md`, `milestones/v1.0-phases/`

**Shipped (Phases 1-4 of 8):**
- Phase 1: Foundation & legal shell (Phaser 4 + Vite + TS, Impressum/Datenschutz, CSP, Cloudflare Pages deploy)
- Phase 2: Level 1 playable offline (overworld, touch controls, IndexedDB save, paper route, Mr. Market boss, Web Share)
- Phase 2.1: Cozy pixel-art pass (Kenney Tiny Town CC0)
- Phase 3: Newspaper verb + coin economy + shop
- Phase 3.1: Zelda camera, juice and repair (code-complete, phone check never done)
- Phase 4: Paper-route glow-up + Mood Pendulum boss (code-complete, phone check never done)

**Not built:** Phase 5 (Story & Heart), Phase 6 (Levels 2-3), Phase 7 (Funnel & Backend), Phase 8 (Launch). 32 of 47 v1.0 requirements were checked off.

**Why it closed:** a feel audit (2026-08-04) and the pivot assessment (2026-10-06) found the adventure play-once, content-heavy and weakly shareable. The user dropped the email/Supabase/Brevo funnel for a credit-only one and chose a mini-game hub after spiking three formats. The tech foundation, legal shell, share flow and art pipeline carry into v2.0, and Level 1 stays playable as a demo inside the hub.

**Lessons:**
- Phases must not be marked complete while their phone (human) checks are pending. The experiential gate was skipped three phases in a row.
- Plans must include real asset production (art + audio), never stubs-for-later.
- Spike a format on a phone before planning it. Every v2.0 format was validated by feel before this roadmap existed.
